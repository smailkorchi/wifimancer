# Code of conduct

This policy covers repository issues, pull requests, discussions and project-managed community spaces.

- Discuss the work directly and respectfully. Critique evidence and code, not people.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish personal information or repeatedly contact someone who has asked you to stop.
- Do not post credentials, private network information or another person's device data.
- Do not request or share instructions for disrupting networks or devices without authorization.
- Keep reports reproducible and distinguish observations, hypotheses and verified results.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close threads, restrict participation or block accounts. Serious or repeated misconduct may result in immediate removal. Ordinary disagreement and good-faith corrections are welcome.

For an immediate platform-abuse or privacy concern, use GitHub's reporting tools on the relevant content. For a moderation request, ask a maintainer to review the thread without reposting sensitive information. Do not make a public accusation containing private evidence; request a private reporting route first. No dedicated private moderation inbox or response-time guarantee is claimed here.

Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
